# Prospective eligibility policy

## Harmonized prospective documentation boundary

Eligibility and scoring remain separate. Synthetic evaluation only.

## Approved trauma-eligibility rule for future reviews

- **Exclude — generic trauma:** Assess the full question response. Exclude only when it contains generic trauma/injury-alert wording without a separate case-specific current symptom, finding, localized injury, or focused diagnostic/CT question. `None of the above`, `Unknown`, and an injury mechanism alone do not supply that additional clinical information.
- **Retain — specific clinical information:** Retain trauma-related orders that separately document any of those clinical details. Such details take precedence over the generic trauma cue in future eligibility review. `ED Injury Alert`, the complete `ED Injury Alert - significant trauma with possible severe intraabdominal injury or abdominal pain` phrase, the same phrase without the alert prefix, and variants ending in `None of the above` are recognition cues only. Trauma-related wording alone is not sufficient for exclusion; finding a phrase does not replace review of the full response.
- **Pending human adjudication:** If the additional wording or eligibility remains unclear, classify the record as `Pending human adjudication`. Record it separately as unresolved; do not silently include it as eligible or classify it as excluded, and do not finalize the eligible denominator until the pending decisions are resolved.
- **Score after eligibility:** Eligibility is separate from 4W scoring and awards no W points. Apply the [canonical Harmonized scoring rules](PROSPECTIVE_4W_RUBRIC.md) to eligible prospective records only after eligibility is resolved. This rule changes no historical v2, refined-v3, or Central-v1 scoring definition, text-cleaning rule, recorded score, or adjudication; those versions remain historical evidence only.

This approved documentation rule has not been clinically or analytically validated, and has not been tested to reproduce all 151 reviewed exclusions from text alone. New cohorts retain their own denominators; approval does not authorize rescoring, rerunning an audit, or changing a historical cohort.
